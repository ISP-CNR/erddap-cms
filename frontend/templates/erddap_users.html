ERDDAP users

Login accounts for ERDDAP itself (used to access private/accessibleTo-restricted datasets), separate from CMS accounts. When someone marks a dataset "Private" without picking specific roles, it defaults to a role unique to that dataset (PRIVATE_<datasetID>) plus ADMIN - assign the dataset-specific role to give access to just that one dataset, or ADMIN to give access to every private dataset.
